# HealthWellbeing teaching snapshot

`healthwellbeing.rda` is a byte-identical copy of the
[dmetar data file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MathiasHarrer/dmetar/38c9b9bb5f85ea49de33e5d2536f6544d5268517/data/HealthWellbeing.rda).
Its MD5 is `0d74964b0b7168379a2b015cb648b326`. The copy bundled here is
distributed under the upstream package's MIT licence, with its
[copyright notice](LICENSE-HealthWellbeing) retained.

The [upstream package metadata](https://github.com/MathiasHarrer/dmetar/blob/38c9b9bb5f85ea49de33e5d2536f6544d5268517/DESCRIPTION)
records the licence for this distribution. The
[dataset documentation](https://dmetar.protectlab.org/reference/healthwellbeing)
credits Mathias Harrer and cites the study review by Ngamaba, Panagioti,
and Armitage (2017). The licence statement applies to the teaching
snapshot distributed by dmetar. Consult the original sources for other
data or materials.
